# AGENTS.md — Decision Records

Decision records are proposals and decision logs written by agents: durable records that preserve rationale, alternatives, consequences, and required verification. Follow the [documentation standard](../../docs/AGENTS.md) and the [record rules](README.md).

**Every new record triggers a supersession check.** Search the active tree for older records covering the same decision or mechanism, classify any full or partial supersession, and archive every qualifying implemented record in the same change. Keep partial supersessions active and cross-linked.

Files under [`archived/`](archived/AGENTS.md) are frozen historical snapshots: never edit them, and never treat them as current authority.
